Educational use only. Equity indexes are noisy, non-stationary, and difficult to forecast. Treat the forward path as a scenario generated by your chosen assumptions, not as investment advice.

How It Works

Holt model: smooths the log-price level and trend, then projects them forward with widening volatility bands.
Drift model: estimates the average and volatility of log returns, then compounds them into a price path.
Validation: the dashboard hides the last N trading days, forecasts that hidden segment, and reports RMSE, MAPE, directional accuracy, and interval coverage against the actual data.
