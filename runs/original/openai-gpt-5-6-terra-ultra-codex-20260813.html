How this dashboard makes a forecast

1Estimate returnsIt converts daily closes to log returns and measures trailing drift and standard deviation over your chosen lookback.
2Choose a central pathThe model projects a capped daily drift. Trend and mean-reversion modes modify the central path—not the observed history.
3Show a rangeVolatility expands the interval with the square root of time. The shaded region is a distributional assumption, not certainty.
P(t) = P₀ · exp(μt ± zσ√t)

Use it as an analysis aid

This is an educational forecasting tool, not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell. It cannot account for news, valuations, rates, dividends, regime changes, or tail events. Historical patterns and backtests do not predict future results.

For a real analysis, import adjusted daily closing prices from a source you trust, inspect the series for gaps or splits, and compare several models. The SEC’s investor guidance on performance claims explains why projections should be interpreted cautiously.
