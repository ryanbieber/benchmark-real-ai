04 / How to read it

A forecast you can take apart

01 — INPUT

Monthly price history

Daily SP500 closes are reduced to the last available observation in each month. Dividends are not included because this is a price index.

02 — SIGNAL

Trend × momentum

The selected lookback estimates a log-price trend. A recent 12-month return adds a simple momentum signal. The blend is deliberately inspectable.

03 — RANGE

Volatility-aware paths

Historical monthly return volatility expands the range with time. The regime control changes the width, not the base-case drift.

base drift = 0.65 × trend + 0.35 × momentum · range = base × exp(± 1.28 × volatility × √time)

This is an educational statistical scenario tool, not investment advice. Forecasts can be wrong, especially around regime changes, shocks, and structural breaks. Validate any decision with your own research and risk limits.
