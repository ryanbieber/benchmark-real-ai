How this forecast is calculated

The dashboard estimates average weekly log return and its standard deviation over your chosen lookback. It compounds the average return forward; the shaded range widens using volatility and the selected normal-distribution confidence level.

  • Trend + volatility: projects the observed average return.
  • Mean reversion: gradually pulls that trend toward zero over the horizon.
Important limitationMarkets can move sharply for reasons this model cannot anticipate. This is a learning tool, not financial advice; validate against current, quality-adjusted data before making any decision.
Forecasts are probabilistic illustrations, not predictions. Demo series is simulated to make the dashboard functional without a data connection.
