Under the hood

Simple enough to challenge.

The model is deliberately modest: it exposes its assumptions so the dashboard is useful for learning, scenario planning, and comparison.

01 / PREPARE

Daily closes

Compute log returns from the selected trailing history window.

02 / ESTIMATE

Drift + risk

Estimate mean return and sample volatility, annualized over 252 sessions.

03 / PROJECT

Scenario band

Compound drift forward; scale the interval with volatility × √time.

Educational scenario model, not investment advice. The S&P 500 series is a price index and excludes dividends. Forecast intervals are model-based, not guarantees; market regimes can change abruptly. Source: S&P Dow Jones Indices LLC via FRED, series SP500.
