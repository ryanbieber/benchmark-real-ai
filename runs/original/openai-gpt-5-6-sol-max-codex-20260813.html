Under the hood

A forecast you can audit

Simple by design: the goal is a defensible baseline and honest range, not false precision.

01

Transform the series

Daily closes become log returns so compounding is handled consistently across the training window.

02

Estimate the path

Choose endpoint drift, a least-squares log trend, or a damped trend that cuts the fitted slope in half.

03

Stress and validate

Scale realized volatility, build a lognormal range, then replay the model through rolling historical origins.

median(h) = S₀ × exp(μh)    range(h) = S₀ × exp(μh ± z · σ√h)
What this model deliberately leaves out

It does not use valuation ratios, earnings forecasts, rates, macro surprises, options-implied volatility, regime switching, or transaction costs. The lognormal assumption also understates fat tails. Treat it as an explainable baseline to compare with richer models—not a trading signal.

Data provenance

Embedded daily S&P 500 close snapshot from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), series SP500. Missing observations are omitted. Forecast dates approximate trading days by excluding weekends.

Educational analytics only. This dashboard is not investment advice, a recommendation, or a promise of future performance. Historical patterns can fail abruptly; the S&P 500 is not directly investable.
