S&P 500 Scenario Forecast

Interactive dashboard for forecasting the S&P 500.

This dashboard loads daily S&P 500 closes from FRED, fits a simple statistical model in the browser, and projects a forward path with confidence bands. Use it to test horizon, model, and lookback assumptions.
