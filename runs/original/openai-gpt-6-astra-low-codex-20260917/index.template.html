3

Check the backtest. Lower error is better. Compare observed coverage with the chosen interval.

Model methodology & limitations

Daily log returns are ln(close / previous close). Historical growth uses their training-window mean; the baseline uses zero; custom growth uses ln(1 + annual growth) / 252. All models estimate daily volatility with the sample standard deviation. At h sessions, median = last close × exp(mean × h), and bounds = median × exp(± z × volatility × √h). These are lognormal, constant-parameter intervals; they omit parameter uncertainty, changing regimes, and heavy tails. They are prediction intervals, not confidence intervals for the mean.

Backtests refit at origins spaced 21 observations apart using only the preceding selected window. Each forecast is checked against the observed close h observations later. Windows may overlap, so results are correlated. Custom scenarios apply your chosen fixed growth to every origin and are scenario checks, not learned forecasts. Trying many settings on this same history is not an independent holdout test. No trading costs or dividend returns are modeled. Forecast x-axis uses trading-session offsets, avoiding invented future exchange dates. A year is approximated as 252 sessions.
