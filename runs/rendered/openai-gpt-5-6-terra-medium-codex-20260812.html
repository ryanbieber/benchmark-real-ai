How to use this

Pick a model and horizon, then compare the projected path with its uncertainty band. Import daily closing prices for a current analysis; the included sample data is synthetic.

Forecasts extrapolate historical patterns and can be wrong—especially around news, policy changes, or market shocks. Do not use this dashboard as a buy/sell signal.
