MODEL NOTES

How this forecast works

  1. Measure. Convert month-end closes in the selected lookback into continuously compounded (log) returns.
  2. Resample. Build thousands of paths by drawing centered historical returns with replacement, retaining the observed distribution’s asymmetry and large moves.
  3. Adjust. Add the selected scenario adjustment to the historical geometric drift. No hidden “AI prediction” is applied.
  4. Summarize. Show the median and selected percentile interval. The backtest rebuilds one-year forecasts using only earlier data.
This is a price-index scenario, not a total-return forecast. It ignores dividends, taxes, fees and intramonth moves. Forecast intervals are conditional on the historical sample and can materially understate future risk.
